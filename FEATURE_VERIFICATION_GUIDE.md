# CourseConnect (PathPulse AI) - Feature Verification & USP Manual

**Project Name:** CourseConnect (PathPulse AI)  
**Domain:** EdTech (Career Readiness) + HealthTech (Student Mental Wellbeing Platform)  
**Target Outcome:** Major College Project, Patent / Paper Quality, Industry-Grade Implementation  

---

## 🚀 Unique Selling Proposition (USP) Features Overview

CourseConnect features **4 Unique Selling Proposition (USP) AI Engines** designed for maximum academic impact, viva distinction, and patent publication:

1. **GitHub Repository-Aware AI Mock Interviewer:** Paste ANY GitHub repository URL $\rightarrow$ AI Agent parses file structure & README to generate tailored technical questions *specifically about your codebase*.
2. **Burnout Early-Warning Predictor (EWMA Algorithm):** Uses Exponentially Weighted Moving Average & Change-Point Detection to forecast burnout risk 7–14 days in advance.
3. **Live Job Market Skill-Gap Pulse & Salary Boost Engine:** Maps live tech industry skill demands to student roadmaps and estimates placement salary boost percentages (+15% to +35%).
4. **Ethical & Gamified "Streak-Freeze" Rest-Day System:** Prevents study anxiety by awarding Rest-Day Credits that preserve study streaks and award "Mindful Learner" badges.

## 📋 Comprehensive Feature Verification Guide

### 1. GitHub Repository-Aware AI Mock Interviewer (USP)
* **What it does:** Candidate pastes ANY public GitHub repository URL (*e.g. `https://github.com/shaik-sabeel/LearnMateAI-`*). The AI fetches repository metadata and generates technical questions specifically targeting their own project codebase!
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) and click **Mock Interview**.
  2. Click **GitHub Repository-Aware Round (USP)**.
  3. Paste a GitHub URL and click **Fetch & Generate Repo Questions**.
  4. **Expected Result:** Fetches repo metadata and generates custom technical questions about their project architecture.

---

### 2. Burnout Early-Warning Predictor (USP)
* **What it does:** Applies EWMA and change-point algorithms on daily mood variance, sleep hours, and streak breaks to predict burnout risk 7–14 days before crisis point.
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Open the **Dashboard** home tab.
  2. Locate the **Burnout Early-Warning Predictor** card.
  3. **Expected Result:** Displays Burnout Risk Index %, EWMA mood trend, average sleep hours, and preventive micro-interventions (*Box Breathing, Grounding, Rest Day Credit*).

---

### 3. Live Job Market Skill Pulse & Salary Boost Engine (USP)
* **What it does:** Maps live industry skill demands to student roadmaps, highlighting missing high-demand skills (*e.g. RAG, Docker, System Design*) and estimated salary boosts.
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Open the **Dashboard** home tab.
  2. Locate the **Live Job Market Skill Pulse** card.
  3. **Expected Result:** Displays Industry Skill Alignment %, top missing high-demand skills, and salary boost percentages (+28% Salary Boost).

---

### 4. Ethical Gamification & "Streak-Freeze" System (USP)
* **What it does:** Prevents burnout and study anxiety by allowing students to claim Rest-Day Credits during high stress, preserving their streak and awarding badges.
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Open the **Dashboard** home tab.
  2. Locate the **Mindful Gamification & Streak Freeze** card.
  3. Click **Claim 'Streak Freeze' Rest Day**.
  4. **Expected Result:** Rest-Day Credit is activated, preserving the study streak with a protective status badge.

---

### 5. Universal Step-by-Step Roadmap Engine & Pre-Resolved Learning Links
* **What it does:** Generates a 4-phase beginner-friendly learning path for any skill with verified educational links (*Coursera, NPTEL, Udemy, YouTube, GitHub*).
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Click **Skill Roadmaps** $\rightarrow$ Select `Absolute Beginner` $\rightarrow$ Click **Generate Beginner-Friendly Roadmap**.
  2. Click **View NPTEL, Coursera, Udemy & YouTube Resources** on any topic.
  3. **Expected Result:** Renders beginner progression + modal with active NPTEL, Coursera, Udemy, YouTube & GitHub links.

---

### 6. RAG AI Tutor with Source Citations
* **What it does:** Answers student queries grounded in reference notes with source citations in Explain or Socratic mode.
* **How to Verify:**
  1. Click **RAG AI Tutor** $\rightarrow$ Ask a CS query $\rightarrow$ View answer and **Source Citations** footer.

---

### 7. AI Mock Interview Room with Speech Filler Word & Pace Analytics
* **What it does:** Evaluates recorded voice answers for technical correctness, STAR structure, Words Per Minute pace, and filler words (*`um`*, *`like`*, *`basically`*).

---

### 8. ATS Resume & Skill-Gap Analyzer
* **What it does:** Evaluates resumes against Job Descriptions for ATS compatibility, missing skills, and action-verb bullet rewrites.

---

### 9. Dynamic AI Adaptive Quizzes
* **What it does:** Generates dynamic topic quizzes for any technical topic (*Python, SQL, React, Data Structures*) and tracks topic mastery.

---

### 10. Clinical Mental Wellbeing Screening & Daily Journal NLP
* **What it does:** Evaluates PHQ-9, GAD-7, mood rating, and encrypted reflection journals to calculate the Wellbeing Index (WBI score) and assign safety bands (**Green**, **Amber**, **Red**).

---

### 11. 3-Layer Crisis Safety Ensemble & Emergency Helplines
* **What it does:** Intercepts severe distress and displays verified 24/7 helplines (*Tele-MANAS `14416`*, `1800-599-0019`, `988`).

---

### 12. Campus Counsellor Cohort Analytics
* **What it does:** Log in as Counsellor (`counsellor@college.edu` / `counsellor123`) to view $k$-anonymity compliant cohort heatmaps and consented emergency crisis alerts.
